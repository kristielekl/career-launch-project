SJSU offers a vast amount of resources to help students with preparing for life after colleges. The resources there can help with finding and preparing for employment, in addition to creating meaningful connections towards success in their futures.

Many of the resources below are just some of the important resources that we believe will help students succeed the most in their future.

SJSU's Digital Career Tools

Handshake

San Jose State University utilizes this as one of their official career platforms, making searching for employment, applying for internships, and helping students to prepare for a full-time job after graduation.

It is also great for making connections with previous alumni and employers interested in hiring SJSU graduates.

Go to Handshake

Hiration

A comprehensive application made to help you improve your interview skills with feedback powered by AI.

Fully customizable and provides tons of useful feedback in preparing you for your future interviews.

Go to Hiration

The Forage

Free virtual job simulatinos that help the individual to develop the necessary skills needed for interviews to improve confidence and develop practical skills in prepartion for future employment.

Go to The Forage
