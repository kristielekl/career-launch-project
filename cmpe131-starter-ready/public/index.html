Welcome to CareerLaunch!

This is an application made for SJSU Students Studying Computer Science/Software Engineering/Computer Engineering (CS/SE/CMPE).

Feel free to take a look at the different web pages for this application and you can refer to the quick overview of all the pages as follows:

Dashboard

The dashboard is implemented to let you see the total number of tasks that you have created as well as their categorized current state:

  • Planned
  • In Progress
  • Completed

You can also view all of your current Upcoming Timeline Tasks, by clicking on the See all tasks hyperlink to redirect you to the Timeline page
